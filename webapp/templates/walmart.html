Walmart needs a quick human check

Walmart uses a robot check for repeated automated visits. Nothing is being downloaded until you complete it once.

  1. Switch to the Chrome window that just opened.
  2. If you see “Robot or human?”, press and hold the button.
  3. Wait until the normal page returns.
  4. Come back here if you like — the download continues by itself.

You do not need to press anything here. This check is not a login; the program only waits for it to clear.
