Advanced

Amazon caps every filter at 100 reviews, so this tool crawls many filter combinations and merges them. Maximum also mines by keyword, which is the only way past the cap on products with thousands of reviews.

Sign in to Amazon to continue

A Chrome window has opened at Amazon's sign-in page. Nothing is downloaded until you are signed in.

  1. Switch to the Chrome window that just opened.
  2. Enter your Amazon email and password.
  3. If Amazon texts or emails you a one-time code (OTP), type it in.
  4. Wait until you land on the normal Amazon page.

You don't need to come back here. The download starts by itself the moment you are signed in.
